Overview & Commitment

At Movies Center (accessible at moviescenter.site), we are committed to upholding transparency and safeguarding your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we may collect, how that data is utilized, and the steps we take to protect your browsing experience.

By using Movies Center, you consent to the data collection and usage practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with any part of this document, please refrain from using our services.

02

Information We Collect

We collect limited information to provide an optimal entertainment discovery experience:

  • Account Information: If you register an account, we securely store your chosen display name, email address, and an encrypted hash of your password. We never store plain-text passwords.
  • User Activity & Watchlists: Movies added to your personal watchlist, ratings, and playback history are stored to synchronize your user profile across devices.
  • Technical Diagnostics & Telemetry: Standard non-identifying telemetry including browser user-agent, device category, screen resolution, operating system, and IP address is processed strictly for security rate limiting and server health monitoring.
03

Cookies & Local Browser Storage

Movies Center utilizes modern client-side storage technologies (including HTTP cookies, browser localStorage, sessionStorage, and Progressive Web App Service Workers) to enhance your site performance:

  • Authentication Tokens: Secure JWT session tokens stored in local storage to keep you logged in between visits.
  • User Preferences: Storing your preferred dark mode theme, volume level, and banner dismissal choices.
  • Offline App Shell: Our Service Worker caches application assets (CSS, JS bundles, icons) locally on your device for instant offline loading.

You can configure your browser to refuse all cookies or notify you when a cookie is sent. Note that disabling cookies may affect certain account features like keeping your watchlist synced.

04

Third-Party Services & External Embeds

Movies Center integrates with specific third-party services to enrich your entertainment catalog experience:

  • The Movie Database (TMDB): Used solely to retrieve publicly available movie information, poster thumbnails, and cast credits.
  • Third-Party Video Streaming Providers: Video streams are delivered inside sandboxed iframes hosted by external third parties. These external providers may place their own cookies or track usage according to their respective privacy policies.
05

Data Security Measures

We implement state-of-the-art security safeguards to protect your personal data:

  • Strict End-to-End Encryption: All network traffic to and from moviescenter.site is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Cryptographic Password Hashing: User passwords are encrypted with strong cryptographic salt and hashing algorithms before entering the database.
  • Zero Data Selling: Movies Center does not sell, trade, rent, or lease personal user data to third-party marketing firms or data brokers.

Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

Depending on your geographical jurisdiction, you may have specific privacy rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of all personal data held about your account.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request the complete deletion of your user account, watch history, and watchlist.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of any inaccurate or outdated information.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our administration team at admin.movies@gmail.com.

07

Children's Online Privacy (COPPA)

Movies Center does not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from individuals under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately, and we will take immediate steps to remove such data from our records.
